Podcast making workflow

Every step from preparing an episode to finished YouTube and Instagram files, ready to post. Each stage is one wib command driven by the episode's episode.json, leaves its work on disk and has a quality gate, so Claude or Codex can pick up any stage and reach the same quality as Episode 01. Works for solo episodes and for a guest in the same room.

  • 12 stages
  • Free tools only
  • Runs on the MacBook Air
  • Claude or Codex can resume any stage
  • Solo or same-room guest
  • Posting and promotion not covered yet

Architecture

Stages run left to right and top to bottom. Each box shows its command and outputs. Orange labels name the files passed between stages. Output2 and the reels reuse Output1's edit and never change its audio.

Approved values

Settings Ayan signed off on during Episode 01. Change them only through the decision log.

Long-form frame
Screen scaled to 3216×2010 on a 3840×2160 ink canvas, WIB wordmark bottom-right in a footer band
Output1 voice
High-pass 80 Hz, light denoise, gentle compression, −14 LUFS
Output2 and reel voice
Tighter compression with warmth and clarity EQ, about −10.5 LUFS, no reverb or denoise
Joins
0.5 s crossfade between sections in Output1, 0.4 s dissolves in Output2, 30 ms audio fades at every cut
Camera switching
Each section opens wide for 3 s, then close-ups of whoever talks; runs under 2 s merge; a wide break in close-ups over 14 s
Zooms
One per agent slide, 1.2x to 1.4x, 2 s ease in and out, every slide box kept in frame
Captions
None burned into long form. An .srt for YouTube. Reels use 3-word kinetic captions
Reel layout
The speaker's face in a ring in their colour, captions in the middle, B-roll card below: screen or the listener's reaction
Reel hook
The first 2 s say the topic and who it's for. Longer reels open with the vertical sting
Encodes
Long form HEVC 30 to 40 Mbps, reels H.264 CRF 16, AAC 48 kHz

Hand-off between Claude and Codex

  1. Read the newest lines of ~/Projects/wib-hq/HANDOFF-LOG.md. Stop if another agent has the same files open.
  2. Log a STARTED line, then check the stage's inputs exist. A missing input means the previous stage isn't done.
  3. Do the steps. Test-render a short slice, check it, then do one full render.
  4. Run the gate. Log DONE with duration, loudness and paths, or PAUSED with what's left.

Before the Episode 02 shoot

  • Fill in episode.json (Yash's name, role and colour, chapter guesses, sting words) with uv run wib new wib-002 --number 02.
  • Pick a sting track and a music bed from the YouTube Audio Library and put them in episodes/wib-002/music/.
  • Give Yash his own mic: earbuds or a lav into his phone's voice recorder.
  • Shoot a 60 s test take with a clap, then run wib ingest, wib review and wib frame to set the framing.
